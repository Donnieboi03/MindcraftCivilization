Manual 3: Crafting
1. Core Objective
To combine materials into new items, tools, and blocks.

2. Key Principles
The Grid: Crafting is done on a grid. The player's inventory has a 2x2 grid. A Crafting Table provides a 3x3 grid, which is required for most items.

Recipes: Items are created by placing materials in specific patterns on the grid. Most recipes are "shaped," meaning the pattern matters. Some are "shapeless."

Recipe Book: Once you have collected the materials for a recipe, it will appear in your Recipe Book. This can be used to auto-fill the crafting grid.

4. Utility Blocks
Crafting Table: Accesses the 3x3 crafting grid.

Furnace: Smelts ores and cooks food using fuel (coal, wood, lava).

Smoker: Cooks food twice as fast as a furnace.

Blast Furnace: Smelts ore twice as fast as a furnace.

Anvil: Repairs and renames items, and combines enchantments.
