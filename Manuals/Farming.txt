Manual 5: Farming
1. Core Objective
To create a renewable and sustainable source of food, crafting materials, and other resources.

2. Key Principles
Crops: Most crops require tilled soil (farmland), a water source nearby, and sufficient light to grow.

Animals: Most passive animals can be bred by feeding them a specific food item. This will produce a baby animal.

Sustainability: A successful farm produces more resources than it consumes (e.g., harvesting wheat yields both wheat and more seeds).

3. Crop Farming
Basic Setup:
Find Water: Locate or place a source of water.

Till Soil: Use a Hoe on dirt or grass blocks within 4 blocks of the water source. The block will turn into farmland. Hydrated farmland is darker.

Plant Seeds: Place seeds (wheat, carrot, potato, beetroot) or the crop itself on the farmland.

Provide Light: Ensure the area is well-lit, either by sunlight or torches, to allow crops to grow at night and prevent mob spawns.

Wait and Harvest: Wait for the crop to fully mature. Break the mature crop block to harvest it. Replant the seeds.

4. Animal Husbandry
Process:
Enclosure: Create a fenced-off area or a pen to keep animals from wandering away.

Lure Animals: Hold their preferred food item in your hand to make them follow you. Lure at least two of the same animal into the enclosure.

Breed: Feed the two adult animals their breeding food. They will enter "love mode" and produce a baby.

Cooldown: There is a cooldown period before the same two animals can be bred again.
