Manual 8: Combat
1. Core Objective
To effectively engage and neutralize hostile creatures (mobs) to ensure survival and gather resources.

2. Key Principles
Awareness: Be aware of your surroundings, especially at night or in dark caves. Listen for mob sounds (zombie groans, skeleton rattles).

Armor: Armor reduces incoming damage. It is crafted from Leather, Iron, Gold, Diamond, or Netherite. A full set consists of a helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots. Iron armor is the standard for reliable protection.

Shield: A shield, when raised (right-click), will block 100% of the damage from a single frontal melee or projectile attack. It is an essential defensive tool.

Movement: Do not stand still. Circle-strafe around enemies to avoid their attacks while landing your own.

Critical Hits: Attacking while falling (e.g., jump-striking) results in a critical hit, dealing bonus damage.

Positioning: Use the environment to your advantage. Fight in open spaces, use corners to hide from skeletons, or build a 3-block-high pillar to stand on to avoid melee attackers.

3. Combat Strategies
Melee Combat (Sword/Axe):

Timing: Wait for your weapon's attack cooldown to reset before swinging again to deal maximum damage.

Sprint-Hitting: Sprinting before an attack adds knockback, pushing the enemy away from you.

Shielding: Block an incoming attack with your shield, then immediately counter-attack while the enemy is recovering.

Ranged Combat (Bow/Crossbow):

Aiming: Lead your shots if the target is moving. Account for gravity by aiming slightly above distant targets.

Kiting: Fire a shot, then retreat while the enemy approaches. Repeat this process to stay out of their melee range.

Elevation: Gaining a high-ground advantage makes it much harder for melee mobs to reach you.

5. Dealing with Groups
Funneling: Retreat to a narrow 1-block wide corridor. This forces mobs to approach you one at a time.

Area of Effect: A sword's sweep attack (when attack cooldown is full) can damage multiple mobs at once.

Prioritization: Eliminate the most dangerous threats first. Ranged mobs (skeletons) and Creepers are usually the highest priority.
