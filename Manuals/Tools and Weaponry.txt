Manual 2: Tools and Weaponry
1. Core Objective
To use tools to efficiently interact with the world (mine, chop, dig) and weaponry to defend against threats.

2. Key Principles
Tool Tiers: Tools and weapons are crafted from different materials, creating a hierarchy of effectiveness and durability.

Durability: Every tool and weapon has a limited number of uses before it breaks and disappears. Higher-tier materials have higher durability.

Effectiveness: Higher-tier tools break blocks faster. Higher-tier weapons deal more damage.

Correct Tool for the Job: Using the correct tool for a block is significantly faster.

Axe: For wood-based blocks (logs, planks, fences).

Pickaxe: For stone-based blocks (stone, cobblestone, ores).

Shovel: For "gravity" blocks (dirt, sand, gravel).

Hoe: For tilling dirt into farmland.

3. Material Tiers (Lowest to Highest)
Tier

Material

Key Characteristics

1

Wood

The starting tier. Very low durability and speed.

2

Stone

Easy to acquire. A significant upgrade over wood.

3

Iron

The standard mid-game tier. Required to mine diamond, gold, redstone.

4

Diamond

Very high durability and speed. Can mine Obsidian.

5

Netherite

The highest tier. Fireproof and slightly faster than diamond. Requires upgrading diamond gear.

Note: Gold tools exist but have very low durability, making them impractical for general use despite high speed.

4. Core Tools
Tool

Primary Use

Axe

Chops wood and other wooden items quickly. Also serves as a high-damage, slow-swinging weapon.

Pickaxe

Mines stone and ores. The most important tool.

Shovel

Digs dirt, sand, gravel, and clay quickly.

Hoe

Tills dirt into farmland for planting seeds.

Shears

Gathers wool from sheep without harming them, harvests leaves.

Flint & Steel

Creates fire.

Bucket

Can transport a single source block of Water or Lava.

5. Core Weaponry
Weapon

Description

Sword

The primary melee weapon. Fast attack speed. A "sweeping" attack can hit multiple enemies.

Axe

A secondary melee weapon. Slower than a sword but deals more damage per hit. Disables shields temporarily.

Bow

A ranged weapon. Requires arrows as ammunition. The longer it is drawn, the more damage and range it has.

Shield

A defensive item held in the off-hand. Blocks all incoming damage from a single frontal attack.

Crossbow

A powerful ranged weapon. Slower to load than a bow but can be pre-loaded.

6. Enchanting
Tools, weapons, and armor can be enchanted using an Enchantment Table to give them special abilities (e.g., increased durability, higher efficiency, more damage). This is an advanced game mechanic.
