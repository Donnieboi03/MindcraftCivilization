Manual 6: Cooking
1. Core Objective
To process raw food items to increase their hunger restoration and saturation values, making them more efficient.

2. Key Principles
Heat Source: Cooking requires a heat source. The most common are the Furnace, Smoker, and Campfire.

Fuel: Furnaces and Smokers require fuel (e.g., coal, wood) to operate. Campfires burn indefinitely.

Benefit: Cooked food restores more hunger points and provides higher "saturation" than raw food. Saturation is a hidden value that determines how quickly the hunger bar depletes. High saturation means you stay full for longer.

4. Cooking Process (Furnace/Smoker)
Place Appliance: Place the Furnace or Smoker on the ground.

Insert Fuel: Add a fuel source (like Coal or Planks) to the bottom slot.

Insert Food: Add the raw food item to the top (input) slot.

Wait: The arrow will fill up, indicating progress.

Collect: Take the cooked food from the right (output) slot.
