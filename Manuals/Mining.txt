Manual 4: Mining
1. Core Objective
To gather stone and mineral resources (ores) from underground.

2. Key Principles
Tool Requirement: Most ores require a pickaxe of a certain tier to be harvested. Using a lower-tier pickaxe will break the ore block without dropping the item.

Y-Level: Ores are most commonly found at specific depths. The Y-coordinate represents vertical position. Y= -64 is the bottom of the world. Y= 320 is the world height limit.

Safety First: Mining is dangerous. Always carry torches to light up areas and prevent mob spawns. Be aware of lava, water, long drops, and gravel/sand, which can fall and suffocate you.

The "Staircase": The standard way to descend to mining levels is to dig a staircase downwards, usually 1 block down for every 2 blocks forward. Do not dig straight down.

4. Mining Techniques
Cave Exploration:

Method: Find a natural cave system and explore it. Place torches as you go to light the way and prevent getting lost (e.g., always place torches on the right wall).

Pros: Fast way to find exposed ores.

Cons: Dangerous due to mobs, lava pools, and sudden drops.

Strip Mining / Branch Mining:

Method: Descend to a target Y-level (e.g., -59 for diamonds). Dig a main tunnel (e.g., 2 blocks high, 1 block wide). From this main tunnel, dig straight branches off to the side every 2-3 blocks.

Pros: Safe, systematic, and highly effective for finding specific ores like diamond.

Cons: Can be time-consuming and produce a lot of non-valuable cobblestone.

5. Smelting
Ores like iron, copper, and gold must be processed in a Furnace to become usable ingots.

Process:

Place Furnace.

Place ore in the top slot.

Place fuel (coal, charcoal, wood, lava bucket) in the bottom slot.

Wait for the process to complete.

Collect the resulting ingot from the output slot.
